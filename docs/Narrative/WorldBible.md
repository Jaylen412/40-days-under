# 40 Days Under: World Bible

Tone: restrained, grounded, lonely. No screen HUD exists anywhere in the story. Information lives on wall gauges, Christian's left-wrist dive watch and the projection inside his dive mask.

## Lab 04

- A refurbished benthic research station anchored 15 meters below the Detroit River, near the Belle Isle / Renaissance Center channel.
- Heavy industrial bulkheads, bolted patch plates, yellow ROV umbilicals, green-tinted freshwater, rusted Great Lakes freighter wrecks nearby for scavenging.
- Air comes from a diesel compressor tethered to a camouflaged surface buoy. When the fuel runs out the scrubbers fail.
- Base systems: desalination unit (20 L of potable water a day, high power draw), solar buoy (weak in overcast storms or when corpses cover the panels), 700 canned meals.
- Opening numbers: diesel tank 2000 L, burning 2 L per hour; 17 hours of decompression before any ascent.
- Lab 04 was a refit of an older research platform. Its legacy codebase is full of patches by people who have since left.

## Christian Thomas

- 28, African American, 5'9" (175.26 cm), lean and athletic, short haircut with 360 waves.
- Born and raised in Detroit. Software and AI engineer in autonomous marine robotics (AUVs and ROVs).
- Contracted on telemetry and sensor-array upgrades for Lab 04 before the outbreak, so he knows its hull, valves and firmware.
- Voice: an engineer talking himself steady. Dry, specific, quiet. He narrates to his log recorder and to the station. Never heroic.
- Gear: charcoal neoprene drysuit pants, modular dive harness, navy marine-robotics utility jacket, dive watch on the left wrist.

## Outbreak timeline

| When | Event |
|---|---|
| Before Day 1 | Outbreak devastates metro Detroit. Christian, mid-contract, is already inside Lab 04. |
| Day 1 | Opening slice: the base is stable, the supply boat is expected. |
| Days 1-20 (Act 1) | Maintenance, hull patching, intake clearing, telemetry debugging, scouting wrecks with a speargun and salvaged ROV parts. |
| Day 21 | The scheduled supply boat does not arrive. |
| Days 21-40 (Act 2) | Isolation tightens. Fuel and morale drop. |
| Day 40 | The hydrophone/radio array intercepts a distress broadcast from a 40-foot Great Lakes trawler 2 km upriver, family aboard, full diesel reserves. They do not know Lab 04 exists. |
| After Day 40 | Branch A (broadcast) or Branch B (the RiverWalk run), then an ending. |

The infected cannot swim. Thousands of buoyant corpses drift in the current, forming the "living ceiling".

## The living ceiling

A shifting, groaning mat of drifting bodies overhead that blocks sunlight, tangles surface buoys and snags divers. Seen from below through the acrylic dome it is silhouettes against pale green light. It moves with the current, thickens and thins, and shades the solar buoy. Treat it as atmosphere and pressure, never as gore close-ups.

## Detroit riverfront landmarks

- Belle Isle: the island at the channel mouth, a dark shape when Christian surfaces.
- Renaissance Center: tower silhouettes through dusk fog across the skyline.
- Milliken State Park harbor: the marina and fuel dock, goal of the Day 40+ run.
- RiverWalk: boardwalk and promenade, overgrown, lamps flickering.
- Ambassador Bridge: viewline in the distance from the dock.
- Windsor skyline: the Canadian shore across the river.

## Ten environmental-storytelling props in Lab 04

1. A mug of cold coffee beside a half-finished firmware printout, dated before the outbreak.
2. A whiteboard with the supply boat schedule, the next date circled twice.
3. A child-sized rain jacket on a hook near the airlock, left by a visiting researcher.
4. A wall-mounted manifold with hand-lettered masking-tape labels over the factory ones.
5. A paper tally of days scratched next to the main viewport by a previous occupant, stopping at 12.
6. A dented speargun with a fresh rubber band tied around the grip.
7. A taped photo of Christian's family on the Detroit People Mover, curling in the humidity.
8. A shelf of zebra mussel shells removed from the intake, labeled by date.
9. A cracked porthole with a patch plate and a smear of sealant fingerprints.
10. A dead handheld radio with an extra battery pack beside it, the dial stuck on a quiet frequency.
