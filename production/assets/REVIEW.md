## Review caveats

- 7.1 adds art-sheet labels and detail panels; its aligned dimensions are approximately, rather than exactly, 16:9.
- 7.2 preserves Christian’s face and waves, lighting and dolly-in, but depicts a sleeveless harness rather than the specified thermal base layer and commercial neck-ring. Treat it as a motion/lighting reference pending costume correction in production.
- 7.3 has stylized visibility and light transmission; actual gameplay should retain the established low visibility.
- 7.4 has the requested descending camera and green murky environment, but the freighter dominates, the lab reads as a compact cabin and two ROV-like forms appear. Reconcile station scale and ROV count during environment blockout.
- 7.5 fictionalizes the Detroit geography and brings corpses into the near foreground. It is atmosphere/layout inspiration, not a location survey.
- Each video runs one frame beyond the nominal requested duration (5.0417 s and 6.0417 s); the requested aspect ratios are exact. No derivative trim was submitted.
